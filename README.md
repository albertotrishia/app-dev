# My Favorite Series: Sherlock

## About the Series

My favorite series is **Sherlock**, a British crime, thriller, suspense, and mystery series starring *Benedict Cumberbatch* as Sherlock Holmes and *Martin Freeman* as Dr. John Watson.

## Story Summary

The series follows Sherlock Holmes, a brilliant detective who solves complicated crimes using his intelligence, observation, and logical reasoning. Together with Dr. John Watson, he investigates mysterious cases and catches dangerous criminals.
